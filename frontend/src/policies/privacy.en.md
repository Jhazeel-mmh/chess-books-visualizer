# Privacy notice

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

This is a translation of the Spanish version, which prevails if they differ.

This is the full privacy notice of Chess Books Visualizer, at {site}, under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.

## 1. Who is responsible

{operator}, at {address}, is responsible for processing your personal data. For anything about it, write to us at {contact}.

## 2. What data we process

- **Your account:** your email address, your language, the date you signed up and whether you confirmed your email. Of your password we keep only an encrypted fingerprint (Argon2), never the password.
- **Your session:** a random identifier kept in a cookie in your browser; we keep only its fingerprint. It expires after {sessionDays} days, or when you sign out.
- **Your library:** the files of the books you upload, their title, file name, digital fingerprint (SHA-256) and the date you declared you have the right to use them; what the service reads in them (moves, diagrams, images of their pages), and what you save while using it: arrows and marks, corrected moves, your progress in problems and training.
- **Your plan:** how many free daily-trainer sessions you used and, if you buy Plus, what Paddle tells us about your subscription: its customer and subscription identifiers, its status and its dates. The data of your purchase (your name, your card, your country and your billing address) is collected and processed by Paddle, not by us.
- **Copyright notices:** if you send a notice, your name, email, phone and address, the rights holder you act for, the work and your signature. If you send a counter-notice, your name, address and phone, the reason and your signature. If a notice disables one of your books, the record of the strike: your account's email, the book's title and fingerprint, and the dates.
- **Technical data:** your connection's IP address and, when you sign in, the email you try with. We keep them only in the server's memory, never on disk, and forget them within an hour at most. We keep no access logs.

We don't process sensitive personal data or financial data (payments are processed by Paddle), and we don't ask for your name except in copyright notices and counter-notices.

## 3. What we use it for

All these purposes are needed to provide the service and meet our obligations, so they don't require your consent:

1. Creating and running your account, confirming your email and resetting your password.
2. Storing your books, reading them and showing them to you, and keeping your progress.
3. Protecting the service: limiting repeated attempts (sign-ins, sign-ups, uploads) and preventing abuse.
4. Handling copyright notices and counter-notices, and applying the repeat-infringement policy, as Mexico's Federal Copyright Law requires.
5. Answering your requests, and telling you about important changes to the service, the [Terms of use](/terms) or this notice.
6. Running your plan: knowing whether you have Plus, applying each plan's limits, opening Paddle's portal for you to manage your subscription, and cancelling it if you delete your account.

**Secondary purpose, ads:** free accounts see ads from Google AdSense on some pages that show no book. We don't give Google your email or anything from your library: Google collects directly from your browser what it uses to show the ads (sections 4 and 8). You can object to personalized ads without it affecting your account (section 6), and Plus has no ads.

We don't use your data for marketing or profiling. We don't look at what is in your books: the service reads them automatically and only to show them to you; when handling a copyright notice we see only titles, file names and fingerprints.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Providers working for us:** the hosting provider the server runs on, and the email provider that delivers our messages. They process the data only to provide that service to us and as we instruct, and may be outside Mexico.
- **Whoever sent a copyright notice:** if you answer with a counter-notice, we send them a copy with your name, address, phone and reason, because the notice and counter-notice process requires it. Likewise, if a notice disables one of your books, we tell you on whose behalf it was sent and about which work.
- **Paddle** (Paddle.com Market Limited, in the United Kingdom, and Paddle.com Inc., in the United States), which sells Plus as merchant of record: if you buy Plus, we give it your email and an identifier of your account, to link the payment to it. Paddle processes the data of your purchase as a controller in its own right, under its [privacy notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).
- **Authorities:** when a law or a competent authority requires it.

These communications don't require your consent because they are needed to provide the service or to comply with the law. We never sell or rent your data.

On the pages with ads of free accounts, **Google** receives directly from your browser your IP address, data about your browser and device, and the page you see, and uses cookies (section 8). It processes them as a controller in its own right: see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 5. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, cancel it or object to its processing (the “ARCO” rights), and to withdraw your consent.

- You can do much of this yourself: see your library, change your password and delete your account from [Your account](/account). Deleting it cancels your data at once, except what section 7 lists.
- For anything else, write to {contact} from your account's email (or explain how you can prove who you are), saying which right you want to exercise and about which data. We will answer within 20 business days and, if it applies, carry it out within the next 15 business days.
- If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can go to the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, the Mexican authority for personal data protection.

## 6. How to limit its use

We don't send you advertising by email. We only write to you about what your account needs: confirming your email, resetting your password, copyright notices, and changes to Plus. You can delete what your browser keeps for this site at any time (section 8).

For ads, you can turn off personalized ones in [Google's ad settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) and, for other vendors, at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/); you can also block third-party cookies in your browser. With Plus you see no ads.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Your account and your library, until you delete your account. An account whose email nobody confirmed is deleted after {unverifiedDays} days.
- Page images, until {renderDays} days after you last opened them; they are made again when needed.
- A book disabled by a notice, with no counter-notice, is deleted after {keepDays} days. So is the library of an account closed for repeat infringement; of that account we keep the email, so that it can't sign up again.
- Notices, counter-notices and the record of strikes are kept even if you delete your account, as long as they are needed to apply the repeat-infringement policy and answer any claims that may arise.
- The data of your plan, as long as your account exists. Paddle keeps that of your purchases under its privacy notice and the tax laws that apply to it.
- Technical data, in the server's memory and for an hour at most.

## 8. Cookies and storage in your browser

- **Session cookie** (`cbv_session`): needed to keep you signed in; without it you can't use your account. It expires after {sessionDays} days, or when you sign out.
- **Local storage** (`localStorage`, keys starting with `cbv.`): your language, how you like to see the board, and which page you were on in each book. It stays in your browser and isn't sent to us; signing out deletes what is yours.

- **Ad cookies**, on free accounts and on the pages with ads: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites; Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners show you ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, your consent is asked first; if you don't give it, the ads aren't personalized. How to avoid them: section 6.
- **Paddle:** its checkout window, which opens only when you buy Plus or manage your subscription, uses its own cookies.

We use no analytics.

## 9. Minors

The service is for people aged 13 or older; those between 13 and 17 use it with the permission of a parent or guardian. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it.

## 10. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you live there, the General Data Protection Regulation also protects you:

- **Legal bases:** performing the contract formed by the [Terms of use](/terms) (your account, your library and Plus), complying with legal obligations (copyright notices), our legitimate interest in protecting the service and applying the repeat-infringement policy, and your consent for cookies and personalized ads, which is asked before they are used and which you can withdraw.
- **Your rights:** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection; write to {contact}. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.
- **Transfers:** your data is processed in Mexico and wherever our providers are, because that is needed to provide the service you ask for.
